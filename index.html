Scout cannot invent a paper — it only retrieves.  ·  Scribe cannot cite anything outside your library — it may emit library IDs and nothing else.  ·  No agent writes to your library — only you do.

What are you researching?

Ask in plain words. Scout searches OpenAlex and brings back real records — it has no ability to generate one.

How this differs

Zotero, Mendeley are trustworthy because they are dumb — they record what you already chose.

SciSpace, ChatGPT are useful because they reason, and untrustworthy for the same reason.

Sanad retrieves first, so the reasoning never touches the citation.

Sources Scout found

Write

Your own words. Type @ to cite a source you approved. Sanad fills in the author and year from the record. It checks your text when you ask and never rewrites it.

What Scribe wrote

Every claim is drawn from the abstract of a source you approved, and carries the library ID it came from. The Auditor has labelled each one.

Trust labels

Verified — its supporting sentence is in the abstract, the claim stays within it, and the reference passed audit.
Reported (not checked) — stated by the source, not independently confirmed.
AI reasoning — the system's own inference. Your judgement needed.
Not found — no reliable source. Never replaced with a guess.
